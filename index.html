Questions students ask

How do I make a project topic original?

Choose a specific user or operating context, measure an outcome, and explain the trade-off your prototype explores. Originality can come from problem framing and evaluation.

What is the difference between a mini and major project?

A mini project proves one focused workflow. A major project connects modules, evaluates the result, and documents deployment and limitations.
